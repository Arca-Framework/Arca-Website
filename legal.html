Privacy

This website has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and doesn't set cookies. It does:

  • Load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Those services receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads, under their own privacy policies.
  • Remember your docs theme (light / dark) in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Arca itself sends no data to ScapeScripts. It has no telemetry and no "phone home". Everything it stores (characters, Rockstar licence and Discord identifiers, inventories, bank records, logs, bans) lives in your server's database. The only outside service it can talk to is one you set up yourself, such as an image host for phone photos (off by default). As the server owner you decide what is kept and for how long, and you are responsible for it towards your players, including any privacy laws that apply to you (such as the GDPR).

Community spaces such as Discord and GitHub are run by those companies under their own privacy policies.
